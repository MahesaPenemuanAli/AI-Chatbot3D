# Nusa landscape loop

## Style Prompt

A quiet cinematic journey over the layered green hills and turquoise coves of
Pulau Padar. Match the user's reference: full-bleed nature motion behind a
dark forest overlay, large white website lettering and a lime accent. The
film itself contains only scenery; navigation, copy and contrast overlays
remain accessible HTML in the website. Use the existing local travel image.

## Colors

- `#071c16`: forest ground; the website supplies its own dark overlay.
- `#f9faf6`: warm white website lettering.
- `#d8f580`: Nusa lime for website accents.
- `#e6efdf`: mint for the following companion section.

## Typography

The video contains no text. The surrounding application uses its local
Manrope and DM Sans fonts.

## Motion

One twelve-second scene. A smooth camera drift returns to exactly its starting
position, so the silent web video loops without a cut. No scene transitions,
flashes, sound, artificial metrics or decorative text. The web player pauses
when offscreen or hidden, offers a pause button, and respects reduced motion.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not include the reference site's branding, presenter or YouTube controls.
- Do not imply that a still-image camera move is new aerial footage.
- Do not embed external media services or audio.
- Do not bake website text or gradients into the video.
