The downloaded binary is unchanged. Nusa AI applies green and cream materials at runtime and adds a blink morph and a small animated mouth. Original idle, wave, nod, and thumbs-up clips drive body movement. The character and all animation clips are served locally; loading the avatar does not contact an external model service.
